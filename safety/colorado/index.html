Colorado Safety Policy

Last updated and in force from: November 20, 2026

This Colorado Safety Policy is part of our Terms of Service for members who live in Colorado. It says what conduct we do not allow, what we check, what we do after a report, and where you can get help. In this policy, "Tune", "we", "us" and "our" mean Tunelab Technologies, Inc., and "you" means a member who lives in Colorado.

1. Conduct that we do not allow

Sections 2.2 to 2.4 of our Terms set the community rules. When you attend a Tune event, these rules apply to you there too. Under these rules, you must not, for example:

  • harass, bully, stalk, threaten, intimidate, defame or harm any person;
  • misrepresent your identity, your age, your relationship status or your intentions, or use the photos of another person;
  • contact a person who you know or believe to be under 18;
  • ask another member for money or for something of value, or use Tune for fraud;
  • share the private information of another person without that person's permission;
  • send sexually explicit content to a person who did not ask for it;
  • share an intimate image of a person without that person's consent;
  • use Tune for a purpose that is harmful or illegal, for example human trafficking.

We also do not allow any conduct that threatens the safety of a person or of the public, on Tune or off it. This includes each act, threat or attempt of homicide, unlawful sexual conduct, assault, kidnapping, stalking, harassment, robbery or theft. It also includes giving a person alcohol or a drug without their knowledge or consent.

2. Background checks

We do not run criminal background checks on our members. We do not check members against sex offender registries. A person cannot use Tune if they committed a crime that involves violence, a threat of violence or sexual misconduct, if they were convicted of such a crime, or if they pled no contest to one. A person who has to register as a sex offender on any state, federal or local register cannot use Tune either. If we learn that this is true of a member, we can close the account without notice.

3. Identity and age

We do not check a member's identity. Each member must be 18 or older. When a person signs up, they give their date of birth, and we do not open an account for a person whose date of birth shows that they are under 18. We do not check that the date of birth is true. Each member also confirms a mobile phone number that can receive text messages.

Before you send your first message, we ask you to complete a face check. It checks that the person who writes is the person in the profile photos. It is not an identity check, an age check or a background check. Section 9 of our Privacy Policy says more about the face check.

4. When we suspend or ban an account

We can use automated tools, which can include artificial intelligence, and people to review reports and content. If we believe that a member broke our Terms or this policy, or that a person is at risk, we can remove content, show an account to fewer people, suspend an account, or ban it. A report can lead to any of these actions. What we do depends on how serious the violation is and on whether it happened before. We can also refuse a person entry to a Tune event, or ask a person to leave it. A member whose account we suspended or banned must not create a new account, unless we give written permission.

5. Appeals

If we suspend your account, you can appeal in the app. We can use automated tools, which can include artificial intelligence, and people to review appeals. A ban is final.

If we show your account to fewer people, you can appeal in the app.

6. When we tell other members

We do not tell other members that we received a report about a member. We can decide not to share information about an action on an account if sharing it could harm the safety or privacy of other members.

7. How to report

You can report a member or content in the app. Report a member who breaks our Terms or this policy, on Tune or off it. Tell us what happened as clearly as you can. You can also write to us through Help in the app. If you are in danger, call 911. If an intimate image of you was shared on Tune without your consent, ask us to remove it at https://tune.dating/take-it-down or by email to take-it-down@tune.dating. You do not need a Tune account for this request. Do not make a report that is false or misleading, or a report for a malicious, biased or other illegitimate reason. Such a report breaks our Terms, and it can lead to action against the member who made it.

8. Sexual conduct without consent

Engaging in sexual conduct with another person without that person's consent violates this safety policy, is against the law, and may result in criminal or civil liability. If this happens to you, you can report the member in the app, and the services in section 9 can help.

9. Help in Colorado

If you are in danger, call 911. These services can help people in Colorado who experience sexual assault, domestic violence or another crime:

  • You Have the Right CO, with information and resources for people who experienced sexual assault, and for their families: https://youhavetherightco.org
  • Violence Free Colorado, with a map of the local programs that help people who experience domestic violence: https://violencefreecolorado.org
  • The Crime Victim Services Directory, a directory of the services across Colorado for people who experience a crime: https://cova.civicore.com/directory
  • 988 Colorado, the Colorado Mental Health Line, for support in a crisis: call or text 988 at any hour, or visit https://www.988colorado.com

10. Our safety measures

These measures lower risk. They do not check a member's identity or background, and they do not promise how a member acts (section 9 of our Terms of Service).

We take these safety measures:

  • We do not open an account for a person whose date of birth shows that they are under 18.
  • Each member confirms a mobile phone number that can receive text messages.
  • Before a member sends their first message, we ask them to complete a face check, which checks that the person who writes is the person in the profile photos.
  • We can use automated tools, which can include artificial intelligence, and people to review reports, and we can remove content, suspend an account or ban it.
  • We can refuse a person entry to a Tune event, or ask a person to leave it.
  • As soon as we can, and no later than 48 hours after we receive a valid request, we remove an intimate image that was shared on Tune without consent.
  • Our safety tips, for when you talk to a person online and when you meet in person, are at https://tune.dating/safety.
