We cannot help you meet people without some data about you, for example your basic profile details and the people you want to meet. Your use of Tune also makes data, for example the people you like and how you use the app, and the insights and inferences that we draw from what you share. The tables below give the details.

How we recommend people. We recommend people with automated systems, which include artificial intelligence. These systems use your profile answers, your photos, your activity, your conversations and the feedback that members give after a date. A pattern of poor date feedback can mean that we show you to fewer people. One poor date does not. We can show you and a person that we recommend what your profiles have in common and why we recommend you to each other. When you match, we can use the same reasons to choose a question from us that starts your chat.

What you can change. In Tuning, you can confirm or reject the traits that we show you. We keep what we learn to keep members safe, and you cannot change it in the app; you can ask us to correct it (section 7).

What we learn improves Tune. We use what we learn to improve Tune, and the systems that recommend people are part of Tune.

Language models. We can use personal data to train and improve language models that we build for Tune. We never sell personal data for this. For consumer health data, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy says how we use it.

Information that identifies nobody. We can make information about members into information that cannot identify a person, and use it to improve Tune. We do not try to identify a person from it, and each company that receives it must keep it that way by contract.

Automated help and review. We use automated systems, which include artificial intelligence, and people to answer your requests and appeals, and to review reports, photos and the text in profiles and moments (teasers). These systems run with our service providers as section 5 says. If you ask whether a person or an artificial intelligence answers you, we tell you.

What we never do. We never sell your personal information, and we never give your data to an advertising platform.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is open, for as long as we need it to provide Tune. While your account is open, we also keep the approximate areas of your past locations.

When you delete your account, other members stop seeing your profile, your chats end, and you are signed out. We delete the text that you wrote sooner than the rest of your account, for example your bio, your answers to prompts, your messages, also for the members who received them, and the text of your feedback after dates. The text of a report, and the part of a chat that a moderation decision used, stay as the list below says. The messages that you sent to us stay until we delete the rest of your data. What you wrote in an appeal that a person has not yet decided stays until a person decides it, and at the latest until we delete the rest of your data. If you sign in again before the day that the app or the account deletion page shows you, your account comes back, without those chats. The text that you wrote outside those chats comes back too, unless we have already deleted it. After that day, we delete the rest of your data.

After we delete your data, we keep only:

  • the information that stops a banned person from opening a new account;
  • a record that a deletion happened, with no name and no contact details;
  • your answer to "Why you're leaving", with the month that you left and general facts about your account, kept apart from your account and with no link to it;
  • the reports and moderation decisions that a later report or a legal claim needs. We also keep the part of a chat that a decision used when it found a breach of our Terms of Service, for two years. For consumer health data in them, we keep only what the health data laws of Washington and Nevada allow;
  • the messages of a conversation that a report holds, until a person decides each report that holds it; then we delete them in our next deletion run;
  • the answers that you chose in your feedback after a date, without its text, under a code that replaces your account, for two years after you gave them;
  • the records of your ended chats, without their words, under a code that replaces your account, for two years after the chat ended, or after the last feedback after a date in that match if that is later;
  • what our systems learned from how you used Tune, under a new code that cannot be linked back to you, to improve the systems that recommend people;
  • a record of the codes sent to your phone number to sign in or to delete your account, and of the codes entered on the account deletion page, which does not show the number, until it expires after a limited time;
  • the record of the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy that you accepted and of your purchases, with a record of the emails that we sent you about them, under codes made from your email address and your phone number that do not show them, for 10 years after your last purchase, or, if you never bought a plan, for 10 years after you accepted the Terms of Service, to meet our legal duties and to set up, use or defend legal claims;
  • a record of each message that you sent to us, without its words: its topic, its dates and what we did, under codes made from your email address and your phone number that do not show them, for 6 years after the message, to keep members safe and to set up, use or defend legal claims;
  • the records that a law requires us to keep, for the time that the law sets, for example the record of a privacy request or an appeal and our answer, which we keep apart from the record of your messages and use only as the law allows, also when we delete the words of the request, and the material of a report that the law requires us to make to the authorities;
  • copies in backups, in our systems' queues and logs, in our photo delivery network and at our service providers, until they expire after a limited time.

The records on this list under a code that replaces your account are still personal data, and this Privacy Policy protects them. For consumer health data in any record on this list, we keep only what the health data laws of Washington and Nevada allow. What our systems learned, under a new code that cannot be linked back to you, is information that cannot identify a person (section 4).

When you change your mode, we delete your matches, the likes that you sent and received, and your chats from before the change, for you and for the other member. If the other member of a chat changes mode, we delete that chat for you in the same way. We keep the messages of a chat that a report holds, until a person decides each report that holds it. A change of mode does not delete reports, moderation decisions or the part of a chat that a moderation decision used.

With those chats, we delete what was shared about those matches in any circle, for example the questions, the answers and the date plans. If your circle ends when you change your mode, each open invite ends as section 3 says, and we delete an open ask about your own profile.

Face verification records have their own rules, in section 9.

We keep a request to remove an image for as long as its case needs.

If you have a plan, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

9. Face verification

Before you send your first message, we ask you to complete a face check. It checks that the person who writes is the person in the profile photos. It is not an identity check, an age check or a background check.

In this section, face data means the photos of your face that you give us for face verification, and any face measurement that the check makes. Some state laws treat face data as biometric identifiers or biometric data. This section is our written policy for face data: why we collect it, who receives it, and how long we keep it before we delete it.

  • We ask for your consent before the camera opens. When you agree in the app, you give us your written consent to the collection, use and disclosure of face data that this section describes. If you do not agree, you can keep using Tune, and only your messages wait for the check.
  • We use face data only for face verification.
  • The app takes photos of your face, and a service of our hosting provider compares them with your own profile photos while the check runs. The service receives them only to run the check for us. Our hosting provider does not use the photos to improve its services.
  • From the check, we keep no image of your face, no face measurement and no similarity score. On our systems, we hold the images only while the check runs.
  • If you ask for help with the check, a person compares the photo that you send with your profile photos.
  • We keep these records of the check, and each has its own deletion rule:
    • your consent: that you agreed, when, and to which version of the notice. We delete it when your account is deleted;
    • the outcome of each attempt, with its reason. We delete it when you are verified, and never later than a limited time after the attempt;
    • for each profile photo, whether it shows one face, several faces or no face, or cannot be read, and whether the check confirmed the photo and how. We delete these records when the photo is removed;
    • the result: that you are verified, and when. We delete it when your account is deleted.
  • After you ask to delete your account, we delete these records sooner than the rest of your account.
  • You can withdraw your consent through Help. We then delete your result, and your messages wait for a new check.
  • We never sell, lease or trade face data, and we do not otherwise profit from it.
  • Apart from the service that compares the photos, we disclose face data only when the law requires it.
  • We protect face data with reasonable care, and at least as well as we protect our other confidential data.
  • We do not use the images or the results of the face check to recommend people.
