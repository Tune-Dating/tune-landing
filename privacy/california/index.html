California Privacy Statement

Last updated and in force from: November 5, 2026

This California Privacy Statement supplements our Privacy Policy. It applies to residents of California. It adds what the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (the "Act"), asks for, and it changes nothing in the Privacy Policy. It uses the terms of the Act, for example "personal information", "sensitive personal information", "sell" and "share", with the meanings that the Act gives them. In this statement, "Tune", "we", "us" and "our" mean Tunelab Technologies, Inc., and "you" means a resident of California who uses Tune.

Sensitive personal information

Some of the personal information that we collect is sensitive personal information:

  • the contents of the messages that you send to other members;
  • your racial or ethnic origin and your religious beliefs, if you add an optional answer that tells them, for example about where your family is from or your religion;
  • information about your sexual orientation or your sex life, for example the genders of the people you want to meet, your relationship intention and the kinds of casual dating that you are open to;
  • information about your health, if you add an optional answer that tells it;
  • the photos of your face that we use for face verification, as the table says under biometric information.

To recommend people to you and you to them, we learn about you from your chats, as the app tells you when you start. We use sensitive personal information only to provide Tune, to keep Tune and its members safe, to improve Tune and to follow the law. Because we use it only for these purposes, we offer no choice to limit its use.

How long we keep personal information

We keep your personal information while your account is open, for as long as we need it to provide Tune. While your account is open, we also keep the approximate areas of your past locations.

When you delete your account, we delete the text that you wrote sooner than the rest of your account. After the day that we show you when you delete it, we delete the rest of your personal information.

After that day, we keep only the records that section 8 of our Privacy Policy lists, for example the information that stops a banned person from opening a new account, the record of the terms that you accepted and of your purchases for 10 years after your last purchase (or, if you never bought a plan, for 10 years after you accepted the terms), the reports and moderation decisions that a later report or a legal claim needs, and copies in backups until they expire after a limited time.

Section 9 of our Privacy Policy gives the deletion rule of each face verification record.

We keep a request to remove an image for as long as its case needs.

Your California rights

As a resident of California, you have these rights under the Act:

  • Know and access. You can ask what personal information we have collected about you, where it comes from, why we use it and who receives it, and you can ask for a copy of the specific pieces of personal information that we have collected about you.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete the personal information that we have collected from you.
  • Correction. You can ask us to correct personal information about you that is not accurate.
  • Opt out of sale and sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, as "Sale and sharing" below says, so you have nothing to opt out of.
  • No discrimination. We do not treat you differently, for example in price or in service, because you use one of these rights.

Section 7 of our Privacy Policy says how to make a request, how we confirm that the request is yours, when we can refuse a request, and how to appeal if we refuse one. Section 13 of our Privacy Policy says how to contact us.

You can also ask a person or a company to make a request for you (an authorized agent). The agent writes to us at the address in section 13 of our Privacy Policy, with your signed permission. Before we act on the request, we can ask you to confirm it and to show that you are the person it names.

Sale and sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we did not in the 12 months before the date at the top of this statement. We never give your data to an advertising platform, and no company receives it from us to show you advertising based on your activity on other apps and websites. So you have nothing to opt out of, and we have no opt-out link. We do not sell or share the personal information of people under 16, and Tune is only for people who are 18 or older.
