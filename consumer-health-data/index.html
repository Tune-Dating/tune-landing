Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated and in force from: November 20, 2026

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy supplements our Privacy Policy. It applies to residents of Washington and Nevada, and to each person whose consumer health data we collect in Washington or Nevada. "Consumer health data" has the meaning that the laws of these states give it. In this policy, "Tune", "we", "us" and "our" mean Tunelab Technologies, Inc.

Photos

We use the results to run face verification and to keep members safe.

Face verification

The face check can collect biometric data, which Washington's law counts as consumer health data. In this policy, face data means the photos of your face that you give us for face verification, and any face measurement that the check makes.

Before you send your first message, we ask you to complete a face check. It checks that the person who writes is the person in the profile photos. It is not an identity check, an age check or a background check.

  • We ask for your consent before the camera opens. If you do not agree, you can keep using Tune, and only your messages wait for the check.
  • The app takes photos of your face, and a service of our hosting and storage provider compares them with your own profile photos while the check runs. Our hosting and storage provider does not use the photos to improve its services.
  • From the check, we keep no image of your face, no face measurement and no similarity score. On our systems, we hold the images only while the check runs.
  • If you ask for help with the check, a person compares the photo that you send with your profile photos.
  • We keep these records of the check, and each has its own deletion rule:
    • your consent: that you agreed, when, and to which version of the notice. We delete it after you ask to delete your account;
    • the outcome of each attempt, with its reason. We delete it when you are verified, and never later than 30 days after the attempt;
    • for each profile photo, whether it shows one face, several faces or no face, or cannot be read, and whether the check confirmed the photo and how. We delete these records when the photo is removed;
    • the result: that you are verified, and when. We delete it after you ask to delete your account.
  • After you ask to delete your account, we delete these records sooner than the rest of your account.
  • You can withdraw your consent through Help. We then delete your result, and your messages wait for a new check.
  • We never sell face data.
  • We do not use the images or the results of the face check to recommend people.

Health information that you choose to share

Some members write about their health in their own words, in their profile, in a message to another member, or in a message to us, for example in a report. We collect this information only because you or another member share it. We use it to show it, to deliver it, to act on it, to keep members safe and to keep the records that the law allows.

We take steps to keep this information out of the information that we use to recommend people and to improve Tune. We do not try to identify a health condition.

We can make information into information that cannot identify a person, as the laws of Washington and Nevada define it, and use it to improve Tune. Then it is no longer consumer health data. We do not try to identify a person from it, and each company that receives it must keep it that way by contract.

What we do not do

We never sell consumer health data, and we never give it to an advertising platform for its ads.

Other companies do not collect consumer health data about you across websites or apps over time through Tune.

We receive your phone's location, or the address, the neighborhood or the ZIP code that you type, only to find your area and to search for places near it, and we keep no precise location.

Where the data comes from, and who receives it

Sources. We collect consumer health data from you and your device, and from other members when they write to you or about you. The results of the checks that this page names come from our own checks.

Recipients. We share consumer health data only as each of these needs it:

  • other members, as you choose, for example the people who see your profile or receive your messages;
  • the besties that a member you match with asks about you, who see the details and photos that your profile shows to other members;
  • service providers that help us run Tune: for face data, hosting and storage and face verification; for the checks of your profile photos, hosting and storage and the artificial intelligence services that check photos; for the health information that you write, hosting and storage, real-time message delivery, email, and artificial intelligence services, which help to keep members safe and to answer requests. Our artificial intelligence services run inside our hosting provider;
  • the recipients of a disclosure that the law requires, such as a court order, or that responds to fraud, harassment or other illegal activity;
  • a buyer or a successor, if Tune is part of a merger, an acquisition or a sale of assets, which must keep the promises of this policy.

For face data, each of these disclosures happens only as section 9 of our Privacy Policy says.

We have no affiliates.

Your rights

You can:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, see it, and get a list of the third parties that received it, with an email address or an online way to contact each one;
  • correct it, as section 7 of our Privacy Policy says;
  • ask us to stop collecting or sharing it;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • ask us to delete it. A copy in a backup, in a queue or a log of our systems, in our photo delivery network or at a service provider ends when it expires at the end of its fixed period. We keep consumer health data after a deletion request only where the law allows it, and we record the reason.

To use a right, ask through Help in the app, email us at support@tune.dating, or write to us at the address under "Contact" below. You do not need to create an account to ask. We confirm who asks before we act on a request. We answer within 45 days after we receive the request. If a request is complex, or if you sent many requests, we can take 45 days more: we tell you so within the first 45 days, with the reason. We delete within 30 days after we confirm who asks, and inside those times. A request is free. We charge a reasonable fee, or refuse to act, only when a request is clearly unfounded, excessive or repetitive.

If we refuse your request, you can appeal it in the same way: say that your message is an appeal. We answer an appeal in writing within 45 days, with our reasons. If we refuse your appeal, you can complain to the Attorney General of Washington, at https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or to the Attorney General of Nevada, at https://ag.nv.gov.

Changes

Before a material change to this policy takes effect, we tell members in the app and by email, and we tell by email each person without an account whose email address we hold. The date at the top shows when the current version took effect. We ask for your consent before we collect, use or share a new category of consumer health data, before we share consumer health data with a third party or an affiliate that this page does not list, and before we use consumer health data for a new purpose.
