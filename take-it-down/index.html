Intimate images shared without consent

If an intimate image of you was shared on Tune without your consent, including an image that software made or changed, you or a person authorized to act for you can ask us to remove it. You do not need a Tune account. Ask by email to take-it-down@tune.dating. That address is only for these requests and for the opt-out of section 14.6. We do not answer other messages sent to it. Your request must include:

  • your physical or electronic signature;
  • information that lets us find the image;
  • a short statement that you believe in good faith that the image was shared without your consent, with any information that helps us to see that;
  • information that lets us contact you.

As soon as we can, and no later than 48 hours after we receive a valid request, we remove the image and make reasonable efforts to find and remove the identical copies of it that we know of.
