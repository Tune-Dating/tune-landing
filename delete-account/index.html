Delete your Tune account

Tune is made by Tunelab Technologies, Inc.

In the app

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear: Settings. Account settings.
  3. Tap Pause or delete account.
  4. Tap Delete account….
  5. Tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Enter the phone number of the account.

Sorry to see you go

Changed your mind? Sign in by {date} and your account is back.

Subscriptions
