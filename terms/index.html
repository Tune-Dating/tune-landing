14.5 Mass filings

If 25 or more similar demands for arbitration are filed against Tune by the same lawyers or organizations, or by lawyers or organizations that work together, and NAM decides that they are a mass filing under its Mass Filing Supplemental Dispute Resolution Rules and Procedures, those rules and the steps below apply.

  • Rounds. The demands go forward in rounds. In the first round, the lawyers for the claimants and the lawyers for Tune each choose 15 demands, 30 in total, and a different arbitrator decides each one. Until a demand is chosen, it is not filed, processed or decided. If demands remain after the first round, up to four more rounds follow. In each later round, each side chooses 5 more demands than in the round before, so that the fifth round has 35 from each side. After the fifth round, the procedural arbitrator decides how many of the remaining demands go forward at a time and how they are chosen, with regard to what the lawyers and NAM can reasonably handle. When fewer demands remain than a round needs, all of the remaining demands go forward in that round. After the first round, the sides can agree to stop the rounds. If they agree, the duty to arbitrate ends for each demand that remains. Either side can then bring that demand in a court that section 16 allows, and the legal deadlines for it stay stopped until 30 days after that agreement.
  • A round binds only the members in it. No award in a round decides the claim of a member outside that round.
  • Mediation. After the first round and before the second, the lawyers for both sides take part in a global mediation before a retired judge, unless they agree otherwise, and Tune pays the mediator. After each later round, the lawyers discuss a further mediation, which takes place if the lawyers for all sides agree.
  • Fees. The fees for a demand in a mass filing are due only when that demand is chosen to go forward. Either side can ask NAM to reduce or defer fees.
  • Deadlines stop. For a member in a mass filing, legal deadlines stop running from the start of the informal step until the member's demand is chosen to go forward.
  • Good faith. You and Tune agree to act in good faith, and agree that these steps are designed to resolve each demand fairly and efficiently. A court that section 16 allows can enforce this section 14.5.
